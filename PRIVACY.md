# Hearthmere Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 17, 2026.*

Hearthmere is a game on Reddit run by Whimsy Ink LLC ("we"). The short version: we keep only what the game needs to save your town and play your stories, we keep it inside Reddit, and we don't sell or share it with anyone.

## 1. What we keep

**If you play logged in to Reddit**, in the storage Reddit gives apps for this community:
- your Reddit user ID (a code like t2_abc123), which Reddit gives the game so it can find your town. The game shows your username to greet you but doesn't store it;
- your town's save: your cottage and what's in it, your town, the things you've made and your crafts' levels and materials, which stories you've started, how far you've got and which you've finished, and how many minutes you listened and when (with your time zone, so evenings fall on your evening), and a random code for each device you've played on, so the same listening is never counted twice;
- listening that is waiting to be added to your save, and your town almanac's daily lines older than two months;
- your favorite stories;
- when you last played, and the results of the regular account check, so we can do what Reddit requires when an account is deleted (section 3);
- a count of your requests in the last minute, kept for two minutes, to stop abuse.

**Your evenings by the fire.** When you're logged in and listen for 5 minutes in an evening, or finish a story, the app notes that evening against your Reddit user ID, to count your evenings in a row (your best run too). To light it, the app keeps tonight's minutes of listening for three days. It also counts how many lamps were lit each evening, as a plain number with no names or IDs, kept 45 days. Your evenings are deleted with your town.

**On your device, logged in or out** (your browser's storage): a copy of your save, your sound and story settings (favorites, shuffle or favorites mode, where you stopped in each story, tonight's listening toward your lamp, which short stories have already filled a jar today, whether the story strip is hidden), the game's small hints (what you've already seen, when it last offered to save your town), and a random device code the game uses to tell its saves apart. These stay on that device, except that the device code also goes into your save when you're logged in (above).

**What we don't collect:** your email, your name, your IP address, your location, contacts, photos, or anything you type, except the lines you choose to write in your town's almanac, which only you can see. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking of our own. The game doesn't contact any website outside Reddit.

## 2. Where it's kept, and who sees it

Your save lives in the storage Reddit provides to apps on its Developer Platform, for the community where you play (r/Hearthmere). Reddit, Inc. hosts the game and runs that storage and the servers. Reddit also collects its own information when you use Reddit, including this game, under Reddit's own Privacy Policy; we don't control what Reddit collects or how it uses it. We use no other services: the game's stories, music and pictures are all inside the app. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone, except where the law requires it.

## 3. How long we keep it

- **Your town** is kept while the game is running, so it's there when you come back, on any device where you're logged in.
- **If you delete your town**, it's removed at once. We keep only the date it was deleted (so an old copy on a device can't bring it back) and when you last played (so the check below can clear that too); tonight's lamp markers expire on their own within three days.
- **If you delete your Reddit account**, a regular check notices and removes everything we hold for that account, the deletion date included, within 30 days.
- **Logged out**, your town is only on your device, and Reddit clears that storage whenever the game is updated. Logging in saves it to your account.
- **The request counter** lasts two minutes.
- **If the game is retired**, the saves go with it.

## 4. Your choices

- **Play logged out**, and we keep nothing about you; your town stays on your device only.
- **Delete your town yourself, any time:** open the ⋯ menu on any Hearthmere post and choose "Delete my Hearthmere town".
- **Ask us** what we hold about you, or to delete it for you: choose "Report a problem with Hearthmere" in the same menu, or message the moderators of r/Hearthmere from your account. We'll answer within 30 days.
- If you're in the EU or UK: we use your save to run the game you chose to play, and the request counter for our legitimate interest in keeping the game working. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

## 5. Children

Hearthmere is for people old enough to use Reddit. We don't knowingly keep information about children under 13; if you believe a child's town is saved, tell the moderators and we'll delete it.
